# CHANGESET V55 R34 → R35 — 05 Sep 2026

R35 is the Kym Add/Edit and large-date readability pass.

- Add/Edit field labels increase to a practical iPad reading size across Budget, Reservations, Itinerary, Calendar, Checklist and Vault.
- Text/select/textarea controls increase to 54px minimum with 17px content; date/datetime controls use a 58px minimum and 19px bold tabular date text.
- Add Expense / Add Reservation step headings and helper copy are larger and easier to scan.
- Expense category, Reservation type and Itinerary travel-type selection tiles are larger, preserving existing semantic colours and selected states.
- Automatic Destination Budget routing confirmation is promoted out of micro-copy.
- Destination Budget date tickets retain their large day numbers and now enlarge FROM/TO, month and year context so repeated stays are harder to confuse.
- Editor footer actions increase to 54px with larger labels.
- No budget allocation logic, calculations, date routing, record mutations, storage, Vault security or menu-only navigation changed.
